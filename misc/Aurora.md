# Aurora

## Overview

Curtains of colored light that ripple across the night sky on worlds that produce their own magic, brightest and most active near the poles.

## Cause

Every magic-producing world radiates magical energy outward from its core (see [Magic](Magic.md#source)). That energy doesn't simply vanish into space — like any source of magic, it has to interact with matter to discharge, the same way a charge dumped into a lattice has to bleed back out (see [Glowgrass](../biomes/plains/Flora/Glowgrass.md), whose seeds glow when they release a magical charge). Passing through the upper atmosphere, the core's radiation briefly charges trace gas and dust particles, which shed that charge back out as light before decaying to baseline. The effect concentrates near the poles, where a world's crust runs thinnest and the core's output is strongest.

## Worlds

- [Moko](../moko/Moko.md) — icy poles make for consistently strong auroras; sailors and pole-dwellers regard them as unremarkable.
- [Cinder](../solar-system/cinder/Cinder.md) — visible above the cloud band Heaven occupies, though the world's own light and haze wash it out for anyone on the surface.
- [Fae](../solar-system/Fae.md) — erratic and colored in ways no other world's aurora is, in keeping with Fae's wild, rule-bending magic.
- [Null](../null/Null.md) — none. Null has no core of its own to radiate outward; the trickle of magic it catches from Moko arrives used and doesn't reach the upper atmosphere the same way.
