# Null Metal

## What is it

Null metal is metal that has been formed with no natural magic lattices, which causes it to have no interaction with magic.

## Properties

Since there is no magical energy, it can't be blocked by magical force, which makes it deadly if it's made into a weapon.

### Superinsulator

It can be made into a superinsulator for magical energy and electrical energy. This is the reason the [Founders](../species/Founders.md) harvested the creatures of [Null](../null/Null.md).

## Forging

This requires the metal to be formed under immense pressure, causing no lattices to be formed.
