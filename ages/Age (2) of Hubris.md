# Age (2) of Hubris

## Start

The Hubris Virus was created by a group of [Ancestors](../species/ancestries/Ancestors.md) coming together across multiple sub-groups and professions. With their expertise over [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md) and the [Founders](../species/Founders.md), they were able to broadcast the virus to save their world and start the [Virus War](../species/Founders.md#virus-war).

## End

Eventually the war brought an end to any active Founders and allowed other kingdoms to thrive. While at the start of this age there was still an understanding of the Founders, magic, and the world as a whole, the power vacuum left by the Founders' fall sent the world into a dark age. Most knowledge of the Founders and Magic Metal was lost, though some of the older kingdoms were able to hold onto some information.
