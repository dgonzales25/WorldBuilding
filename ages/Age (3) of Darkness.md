# Age (3) of Darkness

## Overview

Most magical knowledge was lost during this age due to war, famine, and disease. This is when the [Church of the Missing](../moko/Moko.md#church-of-the-missing) was founded, born from the desire to be free of the war.

Over time, industrialization took its course, and cities, countries, and governments stabilized.
