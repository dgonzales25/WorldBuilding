# World

A [magical universe](misc/Magic.md) with a hidden sci-fi past. Everyone is born with magical power — some can willingly tap into it, others use it passively (barbarians, fighters), and it can be trained to grow more powerful.

[The Founders](species/Founders.md), a race of organic-machine hybrids, once ruled the solar system. Thousands of years ago they harvested people and animals to extract their magic into [Magic Metal](misc/Magic%20Metal.md) using [Fangwurms](biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Fangwurms.md), which acted as a battery to power themselves. A levitating rail network on each world, and ships running between the orbital stations above them, connected their empire until the [Virus War](species/Founders.md#virus-war) tore it apart.
