# Void

## Overview

The Void is a continent with seemingly nothing and is mostly desert. This is one of the first places the [Founders](../../species/Founders.md) tested [Fangwurms](../../biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Fangwurms.md) on. They completely decimated the ecosystem and killed all living magical creatures. With the lack of natural predators, insects have taken over this continent.

The test never really ended. Once the Founders saw how thoroughly Fangwurms could strip a continent bare, Void stopped being an experiment and became the operation — an isolated, already-dead landmass where they could keep breeding and feeding Fangwurms without risking one getting loose somewhere that still mattered. The Fangwurms themselves never left it; the teeth did, but only after passing through the processing station the Founders built right there on Void to refine them into Magic Metal on-site. What shipped out afterward was finished metal, sent on to forges elsewhere on Moko — the one under [Vael](../aethos/vael.md) among them.

## The Quota

[Ancestors](../../species/ancestries/Ancestors.md) who missed a quota, or were being punished, were marked for extraction. Overseers called it a reassignment — a transfer to a frontier colony, a new posting, whatever story fit that week — and put them on a transport that only ever went one way. Nobody boarding it knew "extraction" meant Void, or that Void meant Fangwurms.

Feeding condemned people to Fangwurms on a continent already stripped of its ecosystem solved two problems the same way: it let the Founders keep making Magic Metal without spending down the labor they needed on farms and mines elsewhere, and it kept the Fangwurms somewhere that losing control of them wouldn't cost anything. A continent already dead had nothing left to lose.

## The Silence

Whatever the overseers running the sorting knew, it never made it further down the chain than that — everyone below them only knew that people got sent away, sometimes for cause, sometimes not, and rarely asked twice. The Founders were wiped out in the [Virus War](../../species/Founders.md#virus-war) before the truth could spread on its own, and whatever records existed didn't survive the dark age that followed. What's left of Void's real purpose is a dead ecosystem, a canyon system nobody's willing to fight the insects for, and a name nobody remembers the reason for. Even the [Church of the Missing](../Moko.md#church-of-the-missing), who venerate the Founders as gods waiting to return, doesn't preach this part of them.

## Why Nothing Comes Back

Void didn't start as a name so much as a description, and it outlived the thing it was describing. Every expedition that's tried to resettle the continent since — and there have been enough, chasing the same intact canyon conduit that draws prospectors everywhere else on Moko — has gone quiet within a season. No survivors straggle back with a story, no wreckage washes up on a neighboring coast. Word just stops coming, and eventually people stop expecting it. The name is older than anyone's living memory of why it fits, which hasn't stopped it from fitting every single time.

Nobody left alive knows the actual reason, because nobody left alive knows Void ever had Fangwurms on it — that knowledge died with the Founders and whatever records they kept. Fangwurms don't leave remains behind to tell a story with, so the explanations that survive are the ones people had to invent instead: that the continent is cursed for whatever the Founders did there, that something enormous and starving lives in the interior and the insects are just scavengers picking over its leftovers, that the [Church of the Missing](../Moko.md#church-of-the-missing)'s gods sealed it off for a reason nobody was meant to go looking for. Sailors call the coastline "the mouth," though none of them agree on whose. If anyone's ever pieced together enough fragments of Founder wreckage to guess the truth, they haven't said so out loud. The stories disagree on almost everything except the one detail that keeps turning out true: don't go, and if you go, don't expect to come back.

## The Canyons

Void is the one place on Moko where more than one of these canyons shows up — several converge here (see [Magic Metal — Canyons](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#canyons)), a sign this was a hub rather than just a through-route. That's no coincidence — the processing station was built right at the convergence, so refined Magic Metal could be fed into whichever conduit was headed to the right forge. With no magically sensitive predators left to avoid them, the canyons have become corridors and nesting grounds for Void's insects, which seem untroubled by the faint repulsion that keeps every other living thing off the exposed seam. Nobody's ever mined them out, either — Void's canyons are some of the only ones left on Moko with their conduit still fully intact, for anyone desperate or foolish enough to fight past the insects for it.
