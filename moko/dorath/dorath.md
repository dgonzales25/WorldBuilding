# Dorath - /dɔræθ/

## Overview

This continent is home to two countries [Mysthaven](mysthaven.md) and [Vall](vall.md). The two countries have been locked in a centuries long war.

## The Ridgeline

An arrow-straight ridge of bare black stone runs the length of Dorath, cutting across the border between Vall and Mysthaven (see [Magic Metal — Ridgelines](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#ridgelines)). Neither side has ever fully controlled it — nothing grows on it and it offers no cover, so armies treat it as a landmark more than a battleground, and the front line has drifted back and forth across the same stretch of it for generations. Orm, the one Mysthaven city Vallerain forces ever found before burning it, sat within sight of it.
