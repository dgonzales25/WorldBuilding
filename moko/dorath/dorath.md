# Dorath - /dɔræθ/

## Overview

This continent is home to two countries [Mysthaven](mysthaven.md) and [Vall](vall.md). The two countries have been locked in a centuries long war.

## Founders' Era

Under the Founders, Dorath was the processing land — where the raw material for Magic Metal got turned into something usable. [Blood Elves](../../species/ancestries/Blood%20Elves.md) handled the extraction work directly, and [Dwarves](../../species/ancestries/Dwarves.md) and [Gnomes](../../species/ancestries/Gnomes.md) refined what came out of it, forging Magic Metal and keeping the machinery running that made the process possible. [Aethos](../aethos/aethos.md) supplied the labor and the food to keep it going, and [Void](../void/void.md) was where labor that stopped being useful actually ended up — but the material itself, and the knowledge of what to do with it, belonged to Dorath.

## The Canyon

An arrow-straight canyon runs the length of Dorath, cutting across the border between Vall and Mysthaven (see [Magic Metal — Canyons](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#canyons)). Neither side has ever fully controlled it — the walls are too sheer to hold and the exposed seam along its floor offers no cover, so armies treat it as a landmark more than a battleground, and the front line has drifted back and forth across the same stretch of it for generations. A single fault break partway across offsets the canyon by close to a hundred yards, and the shallower ground at the offset is the only place either side has ever managed to cross in force. Orm, the one Mysthaven city Vallerain forces ever found before burning it, sat within sight of it.
