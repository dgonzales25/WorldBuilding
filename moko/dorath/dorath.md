# Dorath - /dɔræθ/

## Overview

This continent is home to two countries [Mysthaven](mysthaven.md) and [Vall](vall.md). The two countries have been locked in a centuries long war.

## The Canyon

An arrow-straight canyon runs the length of Dorath, cutting across the border between Vall and Mysthaven (see [Magic Metal — Canyons](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#canyons)). Neither side has ever fully controlled it — the walls are too sheer to hold and the exposed seam along its floor offers no cover, so armies treat it as a landmark more than a battleground, and the front line has drifted back and forth across the same stretch of it for generations. A single fault break partway across offsets the canyon by close to a hundred yards, and the shallower ground at the offset is the only place either side has ever managed to cross in force. It's also the last stretch of the canyon in Dorath that hasn't been stripped of its metal, which only sharpens the fighting over it. Orm, the one Mysthaven city Vallerain forces ever found before burning it, sat within sight of it.
