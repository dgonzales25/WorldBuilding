# Aethos

## Overview

Aethos has no unified government and is made up of many different city states.

- [Hexguard](hexguard.md)
- [Vael](vael.md)
- [Raxmar](raxmar.md)
- [Flergburg](flergburg.md)

## Internal Conflict

Due to the ideology differences on how Founder artifacts should be used, there is a lot of political conflict between the cities

## The Canyon

A single, unnaturally straight canyon — bare rock walls with a dark, unweathered seam running along its floor — cuts across Aethos, linking [Hexguard](hexguard.md), [Vael](vael.md), and [Raxmar](raxmar.md) in one continuous line (see [Magic Metal — Canyons](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#canyons)). Vael's Church of the Founders considers it sacred ground and has excavated stretches of the seam looking for relics; [Dranus Dovo](../../characters/dranus_dovo.md) has quietly funded surveys of his own. [Flergburg](flergburg.md) is the one major city not built along it — its Flerg is steered well clear of the canyon on principle, since no one is eager to find out what a creature that size would do with an unclaimed vein of Magic Metal.
