# Null

## Overview

Null is a planet tidally locked with [Moko](../moko/Moko.md). Unlike Moko, Null produces no magical energy of its own. Because it's tidally locked, only the hemisphere facing Moko ever catches a trickle of the magic radiating off its neighbor — and many life forms there, such as [Fangwurms](../biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Fangwurms.md) and [Flergs](../biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Flergs.md), evolved to consume that trickle directly. Others evolved the opposite way, storing no magical energy at all.

## History

The [Founders](../species/Founders.md) harvested Null's magic-resistant creatures to forge [Null Metal](../misc/Null%20Metal.md), the inverse of [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md). They also found Null's native Fangwurms here first, before genetically modifying and relocating them to Moko to produce Magic Metal instead.
