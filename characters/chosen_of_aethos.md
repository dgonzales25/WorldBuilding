# Chosen of Aethos

## Overview

The Chosen of Aethos are the militant arm of the Church of the Founders, based out of [Vael](../moko/aethos/vael.md). Where the Church collects and reveres Founder relics, the Chosen go out and take them — hunting down [Founder](../species/Founders.md) artifacts and [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md) wherever they can be found and hoarding them back to Vael. To the Chosen, artifacts and magic metal aren't just relics to be studied; they're leverage, and the group uses that leverage to buy influence in the constant political conflict between Aethos's city-states.

## Methods

The Chosen don't ask permission. Villages, ruins, and rival city-states that hold Founder tech or magic metal are treated as fair game, and anyone who hides a find from them is treated as an enemy of the Church. They are feared as much as the Church is respected.

## Goals

Control of Aethos, one relic at a time — out-resourcing every city-state's magical arsenal until none of them can stand against the Church's will.

## History

- Ransacked a druidic enclave in [Flergburg](../moko/aethos/flergburg.md) after learning its people were hiding a powerful Founder artifact, killing nearly everyone in the settlement — the massacre [Umbro Augusto](../characters/umbro_augusto.md) survived as a child.
- Later, under the same leadership, captured and killed one of [Dranus Dovo's](../characters/dranus_dovo.md) former business partners, driving a permanent wedge between Dranus and the group.
- [Dranus Dovo](../characters/dranus_dovo.md), still burning from his banishment from [Raxmar](../moko/aethos/raxmar.md), later maneuvers his way to the head of the Chosen — turning the very organization that killed his partner, and years earlier massacred Umbro's family, into his own instrument for controlling Aethos.
