# Isaera

## Overview

Isaera is a [High Elf](../species/ancestries/High%20Elves.md) who leads both the Church of the Founders and the [Chosen of Aethos](../moko/aethos/vael.md#chosen-of-aethos) out of [Vael](../moko/aethos/vael.md), which she rules as a theocracy. She sees no contradiction between the two roles — to her, reverence for the Founders and the will to seize their legacy by force are the same conviction, just aimed in different directions.

## Personality

She rules the Chosen with the same absolute certainty she preaches from the Church's pulpit, and treats any artifact or magic metal outside Vael's collection as an oversight to be corrected.

## History

- Personally led the raid on a druidic enclave in [Flergburg](../moko/aethos/flergburg.md) after learning its people were guarding a map to a hidden supply of magical artifacts. The raid killed nearly everyone in the enclave; Isaera believes it erased all knowledge of the map, not knowing [Umbro Augusto](umbro_augusto.md) escaped as a child carrying it.
- Later captured and killed Talwyn, one of [Dranus Dovo's](dranus_dovo.md) business partners, after his company's dealings in stolen Founder artifacts put them on the Chosen's radar — driving a permanent wedge between Dranus and the Chosen.
- [Dranus Dovo](dranus_dovo.md) has become her most persistent rival for Founder artifacts and magic metal — his fortune lets him outbid or outmaneuver the Chosen as often as they beat him to a find.

## Longevity

As a High Elf, Isaera has led both the Church and the Chosen for generations — far longer than any Human or Dwarf rival could have.
