# Heaven

## Overview

Heaven is home to a militant angelic society built on law, order, and rank — a righteous, rule-bound culture closer to a crusading legion than a paradise. Every angel holds a place in a strict hierarchy, and living up to that place's code is treated as the highest virtue; falling short of it is the deepest shame.

## Society

Angels answer to a chain of command that governs everything from combat doctrine to everyday conduct. Ambition is channeled through merit and duty rather than personal desire — climbing the hierarchy means proving devotion to the law, not breaking it.
