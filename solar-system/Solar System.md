# Solar System

## Overview

Long before the [Founders](../species/Founders.md) built their empire, this system was just a handful of worlds circling a single star. The Founders arrived from beyond it, conquered what they found, and reshaped every world to feed their hunger for magical power extracted as [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md). The [Virus War](../species/Founders.md#virus-war) tore that empire apart; what's left of these worlds is only now being rediscovered.

## Worlds

- [Moko](../moko/Moko.md) — home to a naturally occurring magical core; the Founders found it already teeming with magical life.
- [Null](../null/Null.md) — tidally locked with Moko, and unlike its neighbor, produces no magic of its own.
- [Cinder](cinder/Cinder.md) — a Venus-like world split between two warring societies: [Heaven](cinder/Heaven.md) in the habitable clouds and [Hell](cinder/Hell.md) on the scorched surface.
- [Fae](Fae.md) — a wistful world of fairies and wild magic.
