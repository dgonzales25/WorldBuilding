# Solar System

## Overview

Long before the [Founders](../species/Founders.md) built their empire, this system was just a handful of worlds circling a single star. The Founders arrived from beyond it, conquered what they found, and reshaped every world to feed their hunger for magical power extracted as [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md). The [Virus War](../species/Founders.md#virus-war) tore that empire apart; what's left of these worlds is only now being rediscovered.
