# Null Moon

## Overview

Unlike the other bodies in the [Solar System](Solar%20System.md), Null Moon isn't a natural world — it's a base the [Founders](../species/Founders.md) built. True to its name, it holds none of the ambient magic that saturates the rest of the system, which suited the Founders just fine: they had no innate magical ability of their own and relied entirely on technology and [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md) harvested elsewhere.

## Status

What became of Null Moon after the [Virus War](../species/Founders.md#virus-war) is unknown — whether it went dark with the rest of the Founders' empire, or whether something is still running inside it is a question no one has answered yet.
