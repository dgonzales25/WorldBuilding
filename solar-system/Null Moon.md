# Null Moon

## Overview

Null Moon is a tidally locked moon of [Moko](../moko/Moko.md), close enough to catch a trickle of the magical energy radiating off Moko's Core, but never enough to rival its parent world. The [Founders](../species/Founders.md) built a base here rather than finding one, drawn to just how little ambient magic the moon holds — they had no innate magical ability of their own and relied entirely on technology and [Magic Metal](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md) harvested elsewhere.

## Near Side

Because Null Moon is tidally locked, only the hemisphere facing Moko ever receives that trickle of magic. A band of flora there has evolved to absorb it directly, and both [Flergs](Flergs.md) and [Fangwurms](../biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Fangwurms.md) originate from this side. With so little magical energy reaching this far from the Core, though, both species stayed far smaller than their counterparts back on Moko.

## Status

What became of Null Moon after the [Virus War](../species/Founders.md#virus-war) is unknown — whether it went dark with the rest of the Founders' empire, or whether something is still running inside it is a question no one has answered yet.
