# Hell

## Overview

Hell is a scorched, hostile world where power and cunning are the only currency that matters. It's ruled primarily by devils and demons, though other creatures scrape out a place in its hierarchy too — and the only way to rise is to be stronger or smarter than whoever's currently above you.

## Society

There's no law in Hell beyond what you can enforce yourself. Alliances are transactional, betrayal is expected, and status is never permanent — whatever got you your rank can be used by someone else to take it from you.
