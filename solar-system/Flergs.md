# Flergs

## Overview

Flergs are native to the Moko-facing side of [Null Moon](Null%20Moon.md), where they feed on the trickle of magical energy that reaches the moon from Moko's Core. That trickle is far weaker than the ambient magic on Moko itself, so Flergs never grow as large as similar creatures closer to the Core.
