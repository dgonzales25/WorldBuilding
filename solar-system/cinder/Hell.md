# Hell

## Overview

Hell is the scorched surface of [Cinder](Cinder.md), a world it shares uneasily with [Heaven](Heaven.md) high in the clouds above. Down here, power and cunning are the only currency that matters. Hell is ruled primarily by devils and demons, though other creatures scrape out a place in its hierarchy too — and the only way to rise is to be stronger or smarter than whoever's currently above you.

## Society

There's no law in Hell beyond what you can enforce yourself. Alliances are transactional, betrayal is expected, and status is never permanent — whatever got you your rank can be used by someone else to take it from you.

## The War Above

Hell has fought Heaven for as long as anyone can remember, hungry for the one band of the planet actually fit to live in. No devil has ever held the clouds for long — most can't survive the altitude — but that's never stopped them from trying.
