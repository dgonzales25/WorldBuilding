# Heaven

## Overview

Heaven is a militant angelic society built on law, order, and rank — a righteous, rule-bound culture closer to a crusading legion than a paradise. Its cities float in [Cinder](Cinder.md)'s upper clouds, the only band of the planet temperate enough to live in. Every angel holds a place in a strict hierarchy, and living up to that place's code is treated as the highest virtue; falling short of it is the deepest shame.

## Society

Angels answer to a chain of command that governs everything from combat doctrine to everyday conduct. Ambition is channeled through merit and duty rather than personal desire — climbing the hierarchy means proving devotion to the law, not breaking it.

## The War Below

Heaven has waged open war against [Hell](Hell.md), the civilization clinging to Cinder's scorched surface, for as long as anyone can remember. Wiping Hell out is treated as a holy duty — though Heaven's legions have never been able to hold ground in Hell's heat for long.
