# Cinder

## Overview

Cinder is a Venus-like world: a scorched, cloud-choked surface far too hostile for most life, wrapped in a high atmospheric band that's temperate enough to live in. Its two societies never developed as one people — they've grown into rival civilizations that have never known peace.

## Societies

- [Heaven](Heaven.md) — floating cities in the habitable clouds, ruled by a militant angelic hierarchy.
- [Hell](Hell.md) — the surface, ruled by whichever devil or demon is strong and cunning enough to hold it.

## Wildlife

- [Sky Jellies](../../biomes/eurytopic/fauna/Sky%20jellies.md) — drift through the cloud band alongside Heaven's floating cities.

## The War

Heaven and Hell have been at open war for as long as either side can remember. Heaven considers wiping Hell out a holy duty; Hell wants nothing more than to claim the clouds for itself. Neither side has ever won outright — Hell can't survive long at cloud altitude, and Heaven's legions can't hold ground on a surface hot enough to kill most of them.

## History

Like the rest of the [Solar System](../Solar%20System.md), Cinder was once under the [Founders'](../../species/Founders.md) rule before the [Virus War](../../species/Founders.md#virus-war). Its history since remains largely untold.
