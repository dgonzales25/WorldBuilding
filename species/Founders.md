# Founders

## Overview

The Founders are an ancient alien race of heavily cybernetically augmented beings who had no innate magical ability. They used their extreme intelligence and ingenuity to give themselves eternal life at the cost of others. They harvested the magical power of creatures on the other worlds in the solar system to create Magic Metal, which was used to enhance themselves. They used Fangwurms to extract magical power from living beings.

## History

- First left their world
- Conquered the solar system
- Domesticated planets
- Farmed inhabitants for natural resources
- Introduced vampirism and drugs to increase efficiency
- Conscripted Ancestors to hand-charge [Magic Metal batteries](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#batteries), since the Founders had no magic of their own

## Virus War

The [Hubris Virus](../ages/Age%20%282%29%20of%20Hubris.md), created by a group of [Ancestors](ancestries/Ancestors.md), spread through the Founders' population — those infected focused on spreading it as fast as possible. Some worlds were dominated by one faction or the other; other worlds were fought over until neither faction remained.

EMPs were used to kill opposing Founders, since they'd become reliant on their machines to stay alive. Only those underwater at the time of the EMP were able to survive, but their society was destroyed.

The few remaining Founders and Fangwurms on their homeworld died once they ran out of Magic Metal to make [batteries](../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#batteries). With the other worlds cut off there was no way to bring in more, and the scattered remnants of the empire were never reconnected.
