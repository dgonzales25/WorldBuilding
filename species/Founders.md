# Overview

The Founders are a ancient alien race of heavily cybernetically augmented who had no innate magical ability. They used their extremely intelligence and ingenuity to give themselves eternal life at the cost of others. They harvested the magical power of creatures on the other worlds in the solar system to create Magic Metal. The magic metal was used to create to enhance themselves. They used the fangwurms to extract magical power from living beings.
