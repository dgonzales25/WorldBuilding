# Overview

High Elves are [Ancestors](Ancestors.md), closely related to [Wood Elves](Wood%20Elves.md) and [Blood Elves](Blood%20Elves.md). Under the Founders, they oversaw and administered the labor of the other specialized groups.

Many High Elf communities still carry that old sense of authority, and place heavy value on keeping the bloodline pure — treating mixed heritage as a dilution of the line that once ran Founder society.

Combined with the long elven lifespan, that inherited authority lets High Elves hold positions of power for generations. [Isaera](../../characters/isaera.md), leader of Vael's Church of the Founders and the Chosen of Aethos, is a High Elf — and has led both for far longer than any Human or Dwarf rival could have.

## Society Flaws

Mixed-heritage High Elves are quietly pushed out of positions of authority no matter their ability, while families with the "purest" lines treat that purity as proof of a natural right to rule. Because a single High Elf can outlast several generations of Humans or Dwarves, the same handful of bloodlines hold power for centuries with almost no real turnover — grudges, feuds, and outdated policies never die out, they just get inherited along with the title.
