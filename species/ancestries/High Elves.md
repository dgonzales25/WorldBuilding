# Overview

High Elves are [Ancestors](Ancestors.md), closely related to [Wood Elves](Wood%20Elves.md) and [Blood Elves](Blood%20Elves.md). Under the Founders, they oversaw and administered the labor of the other specialized groups.

Many High Elf communities still carry that old sense of authority, and place heavy value on keeping the bloodline pure — treating mixed heritage as a dilution of the line that once ran Founder society.

Combined with the long elven lifespan, that inherited authority lets High Elves hold positions of power for generations. [Isaera](../../moko/aethos/vael.md#isaera), leader of Vael's Church of the Founders and the Chosen of Aethos, is a High Elf — and has led both for far longer than any Human or Dwarf rival could have.
