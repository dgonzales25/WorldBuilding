# Overview

Orcs are [Ancestors](Ancestors.md), bred as the enforcement arm of the Founders' labor system. When a quota wasn't met, an Orc overseer was the one who marked the name, put the story of "reassignment" together, and walked the condemned to the transport that only ever went one way — see [Void — The Quota](../../moko/void/void.md#the-quota). They never knew where the transports really went either; the silence went all the way up past them too.

## Society Flaws

Being the visible hand of the Quota — not its architects — left Orc society with nowhere to put the blame but on itself. Older generations raised their children to treat hardness as a virtue, teaching them to stop asking what happened to the people marked for "reassignment" long before they were old enough to understand what the silence was protecting them from. That inherited suppression didn't end with the Founders: other Ancestor groups still flinch from Orcs on old instinct, so most Orc communities have kept doing the only trade history handed them — bounty work, debt collection, enforcement for whoever's paying — because it's the one thing nobody else will hire them away from.
