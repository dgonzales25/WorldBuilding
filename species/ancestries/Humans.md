# Overview 
Humans grew farmed and had livestock 

## Society Flaws

Without the Elves' long lifespans or a guild-bound trade like the Dwarves', Human society tends to think in the short term — land and livestock change hands fast, and alliances and rivalries flare up and burn out within a generation. Long-standing agreements with longer-lived neighbors get renegotiated or ignored as soon as the Humans who signed them die off, and that same short lifespan breeds a running resentment toward Elves in particular, who can sit on the same grudge, land claim, or seat of power for longer than most Humans live.
