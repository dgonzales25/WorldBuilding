# Overview 

This is a group that forged magic metal for the founders. Most Dwarves now live in [Vall](../../moko/dorath/vall.md), where they carved their cities into the mountains to escape the cold.

## Society Flaws

Dwarven society was built to forge magic metal for the Founders, and it never fully reckoned with what that meant — the metal is refined from extracted souls, and most Dwarves still treat that as someone else's crime, pointing to the [Blood Elves](Blood%20Elves.md) who handled extraction rather than their own guild's role in finishing the job. Dwarven trades are passed down through rigid guild lines, and a Dwarf born outside the right guild has little path into the more prestigious forges no matter their skill.
