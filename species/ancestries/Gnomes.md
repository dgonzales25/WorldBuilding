# Overview

Gnomes are [Ancestors](Ancestors.md), bred to maintain the Founders' machines — the rail stations, the reactors that powered them, and later the trains and processing equipment on [Void](../../moko/void/void.md) — work that sat alongside [Dwarves'](Dwarves.md) metalforging but never touched the metal itself. Where a Dwarf could tell you what magic metal was capable of, a Gnome was the one who kept the thing running that pushed a charge into it.

Most Gnomes settled in [Dorath](../../moko/dorath/dorath.md) alongside the Dwarves, sharing [Vall's](../../moko/dorath/vall.md) mountain holds — the two trades never touched the same material, but they never worked far apart either.

## Society Flaws

Gnome guilds kept whatever fragments of the Founders' engineering they had left at the end of the Virus War, and instead of pooling them back into something whole, they hoarded them — every guild treating its scraps as leverage over the others rather than pieces of the same lost machine. That rivalry outlived the reason for it: most of what any single guild holds is useless without the pieces the others are sitting on, but centuries of guarding turf means nobody's traded first, and Founder technology has stayed half-understood ever since.
