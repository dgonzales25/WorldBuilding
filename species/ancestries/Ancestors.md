# Overview

All humanoids share ancestors but have specialized to their environment with different characteristics between groups of people. The domestication and modification of their species made many different groups with affiliation to certain climates on [Moko](../../moko/Moko.md). After the [Virus War](../Founders.md#virus-war) many groups intermingled leading to many diverse humanoids.

## Drugs

Drugs have evolved with humanoid. Drugs vary in lots of ways
 - addictiveness
 - personality shifting
 - magicshift

 some can negatively magicshift and make you more relaxed
 other can amp you up and positively magicshift

# Sub groups
Many sub groups of the Ancestors were specialized to do specific tasks within the hierarchy of the [Founders'](../Founders.md) constructed society
* [Dwarves](Dwarves.md) - Refined magic metal
* [Wood Elves](Wood%20Elves.md) - Grew food and drugs
* [Humans](Humans.md) - Farming and livestock
* [Blood Elves](Blood%20Elves.md) - Handled extraction
* [High Elves](High%20Elves.md) - Oversaw and administered the other groups
If quotas weren't met or as punishment they would be sent to become extracted

After the [Virus War](../Founders.md#virus-war) these sub groups had to fill in the missing gaps that their society's lacked due to the control of the Founders.

## Vampirism
A failed experiment made by the founders where the afflicted drink the blood of creatures. After consuming blood they gain a burst of power. Some drugs are made from the blood of vampires. This was not viable to the founders because vampire blood does not refine correctly into magic metal.
