# Overview 
Wood elves tended to the growing of food to feed the other [Ancestor](Ancestors.md) groups — and the drugs. Many now live in [Flergburg](../../moko/aethos/flergburg.md), a city grown on the back of a single massive Flerg.

## Society Flaws

With cultivation this central to Wood Elf life, the drug trade runs through everything. Addiction is common and rarely treated as a problem until it's severe, and the elves who control the more potent, personality-shifting strains hold outsized influence over the rest of Wood Elf society, whether or not they hold any formal title.
