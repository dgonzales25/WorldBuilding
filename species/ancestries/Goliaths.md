# Overview

Goliaths are [Ancestors](Ancestors.md), bred for the heaviest labor the Founders needed: digging the canyon trenches that buried the Magic Metal conduits of the [levitating rail network](../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md#canyons), and hauling raw ore and finished ingots between mines, the [Void](../../moko/void/void.md) processing station, and the forges. Where Orcs enforced the system, Goliaths built and carried it — ordinary excavation, but on a scale and a schedule no other group could sustain.

## Society Flaws

Goliaths were valued for what their bodies could carry, never for anything else, and centuries later that's still the box most other Ancestor groups put them in — muscle for hire, not worth consulting on anything past how much weight it can move. Many Goliath communities have leaned into it rather than fight it, prizing physical toughness above all else and treating anyone who chases a forge, a ledger, or a spellbook instead as having wasted what they were given, which keeps the same narrow trade — and the same worn-down bodies — passed down instead of questioned.
