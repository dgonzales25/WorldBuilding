# Overview 
Glowgrass is a grass that when moved it fires its seed. The seed is magically charged and glows bright green and launched from the plant. This causes a chain reaction to fired off other glowgrass seeds. At night plains with glow grass have waves of green light as plants chain react. 

## Uses
The seeds are made in a flour and used for baking. The flower has a light white green glow to it.
