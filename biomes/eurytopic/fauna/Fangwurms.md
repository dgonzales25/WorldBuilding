# Fangwurms

## Overview

Fangwurms are worm-like creatures that hunt using magical sight. A Fangwurm crushes organic matter with enough force that only solid magical energy remains; this [Magic Metal](../../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md) becomes part of the Fangwurm's own body, and the Fangwurm gains some of the magical properties of whatever it consumed (horizontal gene transfer).

## Origin

Fangwurms are native to the Moko-facing side of [Null Moon](../../../solar-system/Null%20Moon.md), alongside [Flergs](../../../solar-system/Flergs.md). The [Founders](../../../species/Founders.md) brought them to Moko and used them to harvest Magic Metal directly from living creatures — [Void](../../../moko/void/void.md) was one of the first places they tested them, wiping out its ecosystem in the process.
