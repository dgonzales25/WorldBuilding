# Flergs

## Overview

Flergs have a gelatinous exterior with many wisp-like tentacles coming off the main body. They never stop growing and don't age. On their homeworld of [Null](../../../null/Null.md) they're hunted by [Fangwurms](Fangwurms.md), so they don't grow very large there. Some of the original Flergs brought over by the [Founders](../../../species/Founders.md) have grown so large that cities and forests live on them — [Flergburg](../../../moko/aethos/flergburg.md), in [Aethos](../../../moko/aethos/aethos.md), is built entirely on the back of one.

Flergs can't process magic directly and need to absorb it via physical contact. They collect magical plants and fungi and affix the objects to themselves, sometimes absorbing the magical properties of the plant — forming a symbiotic relationship.

## Reproduction

Once a Flerg grows to a certain size, it divides in two through asexual reproduction, splitting its stored magical energy evenly between both halves and starting the growth cycle over again.

Flergs are extremely drawn to [Magic Metal](../../../misc/Magic%20Metal.md), since it's the densest form of magical energy they can absorb. The [Founders](../../../species/Founders.md) discovered that a Flerg given a single piece of Magic Metal large enough can't divide that energy evenly between the two halves it would split into — so it simply stops dividing and keeps growing instead. Giving it a second, equally sized piece of Magic Metal restores the balance and lets it divide again.

Flergs like to rearrange the things stuck to their bodies, slowly shifting them around to try out different styles. Farmers often think of them as pests, since they'll steal plants and equipment. Flergs can be befriended by giving them a magical gift.

When Flergs die, their gelatinous exterior becomes a liquid. This liquid is used as a key ingredient in potion and drug making.
