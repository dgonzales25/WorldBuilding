# Sky Jellies

## Overview

These are jellyfish that can fly. They use magic to convert water vapor in the air into hydrogen gas, which lets them float. They are scared of fire and will explode if they get too close.

## Origin

Sky Jellies originated on [Cinder](../../../solar-system/cinder/Cinder.md), drifting through the same cloud band that [Heaven's](../../../solar-system/cinder/Heaven.md) floating cities occupy.
