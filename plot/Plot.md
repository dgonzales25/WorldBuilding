# General Plot

* Start out with main character watching stars at the South of the planet. They notice two things about things in the night sky. No one believes them. Since this is not a space age society they don't know what a satellite is.
* There are satellites that show up with regular intervals
* There is something between the two planets. Can tell by traveling around and seeing the shift.
* These are both founder space stations in different orbit. One at lagrange 1

* Side characters notice other small things that are odd about nature
* Side character notices how things grow faster in places with lava flows since there is higher magical energy near lava

* Our adventures meet up at the Hexguard college
* Years go by with some small things happening
* They join an adventuring guild to gain some extra gold on the side
* Small contract from former rouge teacher
* Contracts come on a first one to pick it up gets it
* Assassin hit on rouge teacher for swiping some magic metal in the shape of a bone after you come back from the quest (who is assassin?)
* Dies but tells you about the bone
* Police find you at the scene of the crime and try to take you in
* Found guilty of murder and sentenced to execution
* Broken out by resistance
* Help Head Artificer [Dranus Dovo](../characters/dranus_dovo.md) get a stolen magic metal item back from a crazed ex-faculty wizard. This is the illusion teacher who is part rogue. The Artificer gives the party good magical items enchanted with clairvoyance so he can hear and see. This is the final part of act 1.
* Learn that Dranus is attempting to graft a magic metal skeleton to gain magical power
* Attempt to find magic metal before Dranus does
* Dranus isn't focusing on them but rather Vael's supply of magic metal
